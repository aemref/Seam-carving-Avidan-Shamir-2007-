Berkay implemented object removal with masks in `seamcarving/object_removal.py`: removal and protection weights derived from the energy map so that they always dominate it, the choice of seam direction, and the removal loop with optional mask dilation and size restoration. Berkay also wrote the shared visualisation helpers in `seamcarving/viz.py` (energy maps, seam overlays, labelled comparison grids and GIFs) used by every experiment, and the mask painter `tools/mask_painter.py` with which the team painted its removal and protection masks.
