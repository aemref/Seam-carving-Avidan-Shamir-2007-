Furkan implemented seam insertion and enlarging (`seamcarving/enlarge.py`): inserting a set of seams given as a boolean map, k-seam enlarging in width and height with staged steps, and content amplification, together with their unit tests. He wrote `tools/prepare_dataset.py`, which brings every photo to the 800-pixel working size, strips EXIF data, keeps the per-person metadata and draws the dataset overview, and he documented the dataset (Section 5). In experiment D4 he reproduced the paper's Fig. 8: duplicating the best seam again and again creates one smeared band, while duplicating the first k removal seams spreads the new columns over the whole frame, though it still widens objects and bends straight lines that scaling keeps straight. In D7 he showed that seams cut through people and straight lines, and that a protect mask saves the person but moves the distortion into the background. He contributed four photos (manzara, nesne, mimari, yogun) and wrote report Sections 2.4, 5, 6.3 and 7.
