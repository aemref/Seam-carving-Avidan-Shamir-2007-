Ahsen implemented the filtering and energy modules (`seamcarving/filters.py`, `seamcarving/energy.py`): our own NumPy correlation with three border types, the Gaussian kernel, and the energy map with five derivative operators, grey or per-channel colour, optional pre-smoothing and L1 or L2 norm, all checked against OpenCV to 1e-9 in the unit tests. She ran experiment D1, which compares the energy operators and set Sobel as the default, and experiment D8, which measures how noise and pre-filtering change the seams. She contributed four photos with their masks, and wrote the introduction, the energy-map method section, the connection to the course and the energy results.
